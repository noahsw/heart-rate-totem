# Totem v2 roadmap

v1 (four panels, documented in the [build guide](build-guide.md)) is done and festival-tested. This page tracks what v2 is, where each piece lives, and what's still open. Nothing here is tested on a real totem yet.

## What's in v2

### 1. Six-panel round drum (hardware)

Source: [`hardware/v2/build_round.py`](../hardware/v2/build_round.py). Generates with `cd hardware && python3 build.py v2 --renders`.

Six 16x16 panels round a drum (about 306 mm across at the panels, 174 mm tall), instead of v1's four in a tighter ring. No tight bends, readable from every side. The structure changes too:

| Part | Print | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `spine` | PETG | Center tube the pole runs up, floor plate and battery tray in one piece |
| `bushing_half` | PETG, x2 | Round the thin pole tube; the ROCKBROS clamp squeezes their skirt |
| `thumb_nut` | PETG | Screws onto the pole's 3/4"-5 Acme tip and clamps cap and head down |
| `base_half`, `core_half`, `cap_half` | x2 each | Halves split on a panel seam, same part turned 180 degrees |
| `grid_sector` | x6 | One per panel, 60 degrees of the drum |
| `skin_third` | White, x3 | 120 degrees each |
| `ft_top`, `ft_bottom` | | Fit tests for the pole top + nut, and the bushing + clamp |

**Status:** designed, not printed. Print the fit tests first. Final tuned 3MFs go in [`hardware/releases/v2/`](../hardware/releases/v2/).

### 2. The song's BPM beside the heart rates (firmware)

Branch: **`music-bpm`** (ported from biohacker_bro PR #38).

A tempo tracker listens to the kick drum through the board's mics and shows the song's BPM as its own readout, in electric blue: a music note pulsing on the beat where the heart sits, a beat counter on the top row, and the number. With you, a friend and the music all live, the three readouts sit evenly round the ring and drift slowly so every side of the crowd sees all three. Nothing changes until the music locks on.

**Status:** host tests pass and it compiles for the ESP32-S3. Never run on a board, never heard real music. Open questions: real crowds, claps, and build-ups with no kick.

To try it: `git checkout music-bpm`, flash, and set `MUSIC_TEST_MODE 1` in section 10 of `totem_config.h` to see the tracker's guess and confidence instead of the heart rate.

## Firmware work for six panels

Most of the firmware is written against `FACE_COUNT`, so the first step is small; the rest is checking and tuning on the real drum.

- [ ] `FACE_COUNT 6` in `totem_config.h` (and a `TOTEM_DRUM` or similar switch so v1 builds keep working). `RING_COLS` becomes 96.
- [ ] **Power.** 1536 LEDs instead of 1024. `LED_MAX_MA` stays the battery's limit, so each LED gets about two thirds of v1's current at the same setting: check brightness and battery life, and whether the board's 4-pin output and one barrier strip feed are enough or the chain needs power injected at panel 4.
- [ ] **Friend faces.** v1 alternates you / friend on opposite pairs (`f += 2`). With six faces that's 3 + 3, which works, but check it reads well. With music BPM it's 2 + 2 + 2.
- [ ] **Shows.** The spin, the phrase scroll and the Queen Mary are written for a ring of `RING_COLS` columns; check timing (a lap is 50% longer) and the phrase seam.
- [ ] **Orientation test** and the LED map tests (`make -C tests` already sweeps every `PANEL_*`/`RING_*` combination; add `FACE_COUNT 6` to the sweep).
- [ ] **Antenna.** The board sits in a bigger drum with a PETG spine down the middle; check Bluetooth range to the strap and phone.
- [ ] **Wiring.** Where the board, battery tray and barrier strip sit in the new layout, and the order panels go round. Update the wiring diagram.
